## Source facts (from IMD brief)

- Season: Oct–Dec 2026; TN LPA **443.3 mm** (1971–2020)
- Oct normal **172.0 mm** · Nov **181.9 mm** · Dec **89.4 mm**
- Above-normal max & min temperatures likely statewide each month
- El Niño likely during NE monsoon; IOD outlook mixed/neutral per models
- Forecasts are **probability / tercile** outlooks — not day-to-day warnings
