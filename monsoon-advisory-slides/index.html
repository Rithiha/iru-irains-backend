Public advisory · Act on your region

What families & farmers should do

El Niño is likely during this NE monsoon. Use this checklist — not panic, preparedness.

If you live in North TN

  • Conserve water early — tanks & household storage
  • Prefer short-duration / drought-tolerant crops where advised
  • Expect warmer nights; plan outdoor work for cooler hours
  • Do not wait for late-season catch-up rain in December

If you live in South / West TN

  • Clear drains before November peak spells
  • Move valuables & livestock from low-lying ground
  • Follow district flood & landslide alerts promptly
  • Avoid crossing flowing water during heavy rain

Everyone, statewide

Days and nights are likely warmer than normal in Oct, Nov & Dec.

Stay updated IMD · District Collectorate · Local radio

Seasonal outlooks are probability-based. Short-range warnings can change — verify before major decisions.
